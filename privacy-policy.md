# Privacy Policy for Fund-It

**Last updated: April 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Fund-It ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Fund-It mobile application.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Email address** — collected at account registration for authentication and account recovery.
- **Username** — a display name you choose during signup, stored publicly within the app.
- **Phone number** — optionally provided in profile settings.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Donation amounts** — recorded when you make a donation through the app via PayPal.
- **Votes** — your charity votes are stored to enforce voting rules (one vote per period).
- **Push notification token** — collected when you grant notification permissions, used only to send you Fund-It notifications.

---

## How We Use Your Information

- To create and manage your account.
- To process and record donations.
- To send push notifications about voting results and donation receipts (only if you have granted permission).
- To enforce fairness rules in the voting system.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

---

## Third-Party Services

Fund-It uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

- **Supabase** (database and authentication) — [https://supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **PayPal** (donation processing) — [https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/privacy-full](https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/privacy-full)
- **Expo / Expo Push Notifications** — [https://expo.dev/privacy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

---

## Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Your account credentials are managed by Supabase Auth and are never stored in plain text.

---

## Your Rights

- **Access & correction** — you can view and update your profile information within the app.
- **Account deletion** — you can permanently delete your account from the Settings screen. This removes your profile, votes, and associated data from our systems.

---

## Children's Privacy

Fund-It is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
